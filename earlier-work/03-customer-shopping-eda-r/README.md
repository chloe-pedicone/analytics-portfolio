# Customer shopping behavior EDA (R)

**Type:** Kaggle notebook (May 2023) · **Tools:** R (tidyverse, data.table, ggplot2, GGally)
**Notebook:** [`customer-shopping-eda.ipynb`](customer-shopping-eda.ipynb) (R kernel) · [Original on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/code/yangle2912/eda-customer-data-analysis-by-r)
**Data:** Public Kaggle dataset of 99,457 transactions from 10 shopping malls in Istanbul (2021–2023).

## What I did
- Cleaned the data: parsed dates, derived total sales, bucketed age groups.
- Wrote descriptive statistics, variability measures, box plots, histograms and faceted charts by gender, age, category, mall and payment method.
- Built correlation views and tested 4 regression hypotheses: whether age and gender, payment method, mall location and product category each affect purchase behavior.
